# Add-An-Ad – Web-Based Advertising Agency Management System

## Project Overview

Add-An-Ad is a web-based advertising agency management system developed as a group project for SE2030 – Software Engineering (Year 2, Semester 1, 2026) at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT).

The system supports client management, advertising campaigns, marketing analytics, finance, communication, and task coordination.

## My Contribution – Marketing & Analytics Module

**Role:** Marketing & Analytics Specialist / Module Developer

My contributions included:
- Developing the Marketing & Analytics Module.
- Tracking campaign performance metrics, including views and clicks.
- Presenting campaign progress, performance summaries, and remarks.
- Providing performance information for clients and agency staff.
- Contributing to campaign goals and scheduling functionality.
- Collaborating with teammates to integrate the module into the system.

Campaign performance data was recorded within the application for demonstration purposes rather than collected from live advertising platforms.

## Main Features

- Client registration and account approval
- Advertising campaign creation and management
- Campaign performance tracking and analytics
- Staff task management
- Invoicing and payment recording
- Client messaging, notifications, and reviews
